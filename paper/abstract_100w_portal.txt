Composing pairwise-consistent biological ontology crosswalks (SSSOM) into four-to-six-ontology hubs triggers global unsatisfiability cascades (+63.8% to +125.4% emergent unsatisfiable classes) and up to 234,152 uncurated intra-ontology subsumptions across 188,030 classes. We present CLASP-ONT, a CPU-only verification and repair engine combining two-tier conjunctive Horn/QuickXplain core extraction with Answer Set Programming Counterexample-Guided Abstraction Refinement over a five-element subsumption poset. Unlike LogMap or binary deletion, CLASP-ONT certifies zero post-repair violations across clinical, oncology, and plant hubs within 4.3 GB RAM, eliminates 61.7%--82.5% of mapping deletions, improves OAEI Bio-ML 2026 equivalence F1 from 0.606 to 0.663, and exports KGCL patches.
