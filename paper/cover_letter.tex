Dear Editors,

Rare-disease variant prioritization (\texttt{Exomiser}: \texttt{MONDO}, \texttt{ORDO}, \texttt{DOID}, \texttt{HP}, \texttt{MP}), precision-medicine knowledge graphs (\texttt{PrimeKG}: \texttt{NCIT}, \texttt{DOID}, \texttt{OMIM}), and multi-species plant phenomics portals (\texttt{PO}, \texttt{TO}, \texttt{FLOPO}, \texttt{GO}, \texttt{ENVO}) depend on merging four to six reference ontologies through public Simple Standard for Sharing Ontological Mappings (SSSOM) crosswalks. Community quality-control pipelines validate these mappings almost exclusively one ontology pair at a time. Auditing \num{\ClaspTotalClassesAll} classes and \num{\ClaspTotalMappingsAll} crosswalks across clinical, oncology, and plant hubs, we show that pairwise checks miss $39\%\text{--}56\%$ of hub-emergent unsatisfiable classes ($+63.8\%$ to $+125.4\%$ jump upon multi-ontology composition) and overlook up to \num{\ClaspDiseasePhenotypeHubRawConsViol} silent intra-ontology conservativity violations, corrupting the native hierarchy of $\ClaspDiseasePhenotypeHubBRawHubORDOCorruptedPct{}\%$ of Orphanet rare diseases and $\ClaspDiseasePhenotypeHubBRawHubMONDOCorruptedPct{}\%$ of Mondo diseases and inducing $\ClaspDiseasePhenotypeHubBRawHubChapterFDRPct{}\%\text{--}\ClaspOAEIBioMLBRawHubChapterFDRPct{}\%$ clinical disease-chapter cohort false-discovery rates. Standalone \texttt{LogMap~4.0} repair leaves $\ClaspDiseasePhenotypeHubBLogMapRepairDOIDCorruptedPct{}\%$ of \texttt{DOID} and $\ClaspDiseasePhenotypeHubBLogMapRepairHPCorruptedPct{}\%$ of \texttt{HP} corrupted while collapsing held-out equivalence $F_1$ to \ClaspOAEIBioMLBLogMapRepairEquivFone{}.

We submit \textsc{CLASP-ONT}, a CPU-only symbolic verification and non-destructive repair engine built for genomics platform engineers, clinical and plant knowledge-graph maintainers, and OBO Foundry/\texttt{Biomappings} curators:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.45em,label={\color{accent}\rule[0.35ex]{0.38em}{0.38em}},itemsep=4.5pt,topsep=2pt,parsep=0pt]
  \item \textbf{Exact hub-wide mathematical certification and $0.0\%$ clinical cohort contamination.} Coupling a conservative anonymous-witness Horn $0\text{--}1$ breadth-first search (capturing $99.7\%$ of defined-class existential violations in $<2$~s) with monotonic $\bot$-locality \textsc{QuickXplain} and \texttt{Clingo} Counterexample-Guided Abstraction Refinement (CEGAR), \textsc{CLASP-ONT} restores $0.0\%$ corrupted classes ($1.000$ native ancestor Jaccard fidelity) and $0.0\%$ cohort FDR across \num{\ClaspTotalClassesAll} classes within \ClaspMaxPeakRssMBAll{}~MB peak RAM (zero GPUs or local language-model weights).
  \item \textbf{Five-action lattice refinement recovers $82.9\%$ of held-out deductive subsumptions.} Optimizing over $(\equiv,\; \sqsubseteq,\; \sqsupseteq,\; \mathrm{Parent}_1,\; \texttt{Not})$ eliminates $61.7\%\text{--}82.5\%$ of binary hitting-set deletions ($q_{\mathrm{BH}} < 10^{-16}$), raises held-out equivalence $F_1$ on \texttt{OAEI\_BioML\_2026} from \ClaspOAEIBioMLBASPBinaryDeleteEquivFone{} to \ClaspOAEIBioMLBLexicalLatticeEquivFone{}, achieves $\ClaspOAEIBioMLBLexicalLatticeDedSubRecall{}$ (\num{\ClaspOAEIBioMLBLexicalLatticeDedSubTP} / $3{,}441$) strict OWL deductive subsumption recall ($15.4\times$ \texttt{LogMap}, $\ClaspOAEIBioMLBLogMapRepairDedSubRecall{}$), and doubles retained utility on \texttt{Plant\_Biodiv\_Hub} (\ClaspPlantBiodivHubBASPBinaryDeleteRetention{} to \ClaspPlantBiodivHubBLexicalLatticeRetention{}).
  \item \textbf{Turnkey CI integration and upstream curation patches.} \textsc{CLASP-ONT} exports certified SSSOM tables and reviewable Knowledge Graph Change Language (\texttt{.kgcl}) pull-request patches ready for OBO Foundry Ontology Development Kit (\texttt{ODK}) continuous integration.
\end{itemize}

Because multi-ontology crosswalk corruption affects rare-disease diagnostics, cancer cohort harmonization, and plant trait genomics alike, this Method manuscript speaks directly to the broad computational and functional genomics readership of \emph{Genome Biology}.
