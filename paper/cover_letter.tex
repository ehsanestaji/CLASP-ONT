\textbf{To the Editors, \textit{Genome Biology}}\\[0.3em]
\textbf{Re: Method Manuscript Submission: ``Pairwise Valid, Globally Broken: Non-Destructive Lattice Repair and Certification of Multi-Ontology Biological Crosswalk Hubs''}

Dear Editors,

Please consider our Method manuscript, \textit{``Pairwise Valid, Globally Broken: Non-Destructive Lattice Repair and Certification of Multi-Ontology Biological Crosswalk Hubs,''} for publication in \textit{Genome Biology}.

Rare-disease variant prioritization (\texttt{MONDO}, \texttt{ORDO}, \texttt{DOID}, \texttt{HP}, \texttt{MP}), translational oncology knowledge graphs (\texttt{NCIT}, \texttt{DOID}, \texttt{OMIM}), and multi-species plant phenomics portals (\texttt{PO}, \texttt{TO}, \texttt{FLOPO}, \texttt{GO}, \texttt{ENVO}) depend on merging four to six ontologies through public Simple Standard for Sharing Ontological Mappings (SSSOM) crosswalks. Current quality-control pipelines validate mappings only one ontology pair at a time. Auditing \num{\ClaspTotalClassesAll} classes and \num{\ClaspTotalMappingsAll} crosswalks across clinical, oncology, and plant hubs, we show that pairwise checks miss roughly half of all hub-emergent unsatisfiable classes ($+63.8\%$ to $+125.4\%$ increase upon multi-ontology composition) and overlook up to \num{\ClaspDiseasePhenotypeHubRawConsViol} silent intra-ontology conservativity violations triggered by cross-ontology OWL~2~EL defined-class intersections (e.g., equating malignant subtypes with benign neoplasms or completing foreign anatomical intersection axioms). Standalone \texttt{LogMap~4.0} repair fails to certify any multi-ontology hub, leaving up to \num{\ClaspDiseasePhenotypeHubBLogMapRepairConsViol} conservativity violations and \num{\ClaspPlantBiodivHubBLogMapRepairUnsat} unsatisfiable classes unresolved while collapsing held-out equivalence $F_1$ to \ClaspOAEIBioMLBLogMapRepairEquivFone{}.

To solve this infrastructure bottleneck without severing valid cross-species and cross-domain bridges, we developed \textsc{CLASP-ONT}, a CPU-only verification and non-destructive repair engine tailored for genomics platform builders, clinical knowledge-graph engineers, plant trait consortia, and OBO Foundry/\texttt{Biomappings} curators. Rather than deleting contested equivalences, \textsc{CLASP-ONT} optimizes over a five-element subsumption refinement poset ($\equiv$, $\sqsubseteq$, $\sqsupseteq$, ancestor weakening, and negative curation) via two-tier conjunctive Horn $0\text{--}1$ breadth-first search, monotonic $\bot$-locality \textsc{QuickXplain}, and \texttt{Clingo} Counterexample-Guided Abstraction Refinement (CEGAR). Across all four suites, \textsc{CLASP-ONT}:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em,itemsep=1.5pt,topsep=1.5pt]
  \item Mathematically certifies exact zero post-repair unsatisfiable classes ($|\mathrm{Unsat}_{\Delta}| = 0$) and zero conservativity violations ($|\mathrm{ConsViol}| = 0$) across \num{\ClaspTotalClassesAll} classes within \ClaspMaxPeakRssMBAll{}~MB peak RAM on a standard laptop CPU (zero GPUs or local language-model weights);
  \item Eliminates $61.7\%$ to $82.5\%$ of binary hitting-set deletions ($q_{\mathrm{BH}} < 10^{-16}$), raises held-out equivalence $F_1$ on \texttt{OAEI\_BioML\_2026} from \ClaspOAEIBioMLBASPBinaryDeleteEquivFone{} to \ClaspOAEIBioMLBLexicalLatticeEquivFone{}, doubles retained utility on \texttt{Plant\_Biodiv\_Hub} (\ClaspPlantBiodivHubBASPBinaryDeleteRetention{} to \ClaspPlantBiodivHubBLexicalLatticeRetention{}), and preserves $+48{,}863$ certified cross-species entailments on \texttt{Disease\_Phenotype\_Hub};
  \item Exports ready-to-merge SSSOM crosswalks and Knowledge Graph Change Language (KGCL) pull-request patches directly compatible with OBO Foundry Ontology Development Kit (\texttt{ODK}) continuous integration.
\end{enumerate}
